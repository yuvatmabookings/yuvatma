🎒 Guidelines & Eligibility

  • Eligibility & Age Limit: Exclusively for Madhwa youth of age 18–30.
  • Valid Identification Proof: Valid government photo ID proof required: Aadhaar / Driving License / Voter ID.
  • Non-Refundable: Once booking is confirmed, it is non-refundable. But the booking can be transferred to another eligible youth.
  • Zero Upfront Payment: Register first with your valid ID proof. The payment link will be sent to your email & WhatsApp after ID verification.
  • Attire: Traditional, modest Indian clothing suitable for temple premises and Goshala seva.
  • Essentials: Reusable water bottle, personal toiletries, and any personal routine medications.
  • Weather Gear: A light jacket or shawl for the night CampFire and cool morning breeze.
  • Mindset: Enthusiastic spirit for Go Seva, cow product making workshop, bhajans, and collaborative Madhwa youth fellowship!
