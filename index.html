Trip Itinerary

2 Days of Devotion, Seva & CampFire

A balanced itinerary featuring Go Seva, CampFire, Parayana, Bhajan, and authentic regional meals.

05:30 AM

Assembly & Volvo Bus Departure

Meet at designated pickup points in Bengaluru. Board our comfortable luxury Volvo bus. Journey begins with prayers and sankirtan.

08:30 AM

Satvik Breakfast Stop

Stopover on highway for hot traditional breakfast, coffee, and tea.

01:00 PM

Arrival at Yalagur & Check-In

Reach Sri Kshetra Yalagur. Room allotment, freshening up, and warm reception.

01:30 PM

Uttara Karnataka Rotti Uta (Lunch)

Authentic North Karnataka feast: hot Jolada Rotti, Yennegai Palya, Shenga Chutney Pudi, fresh butter, curd, and satvik delicacies.

04:00 PM

Youth Introduction Circle & Orientation

Ice-breaking session, yatra overview, and introducing our GoSeva - 3.0 mission.

05:30 PM

Temple Darshan & Archana

Special coordinated darshan of Sri Yalagureshwara with archana, sankalpa, and sacred blessings.

07:00 PM

Grand Deepotsava & Bhajan Sandhya

Lighting lamps, soulful musical sankirtan, harmonium, and collective devotional singing.

08:45 PM

Wholesome Satvik Dinner

Nourishing dinner prepared with purity and devotion.

09:30 PM

🔥 CampFire Under the Stars

Youth bonfire circle under the open night sky: inspirational sharing, acoustic bhajans, life stories, and quiet stargazing.

11:00 PM

Rest & Overnight Stay

Restful sleep in clean, hygienic accommodation.

05:45 AM

Suprabhata, Yoga & Morning Meditation

Wake up to sacred chanting, refreshing morning yoga stretches, and peaceful dhyana.

07:15 AM

Temple Parikrama & Hot Breakfast

Morning temple circumambulation followed by hearty satvik breakfast.

08:30 AM

🐄 Dedicated Go Seva at Goshala

Gau puja, feeding sacred cows with fresh grass & jaggery, grooming, cleaning the goshala, and experiencing divine grace.

10:45 AM

📖 Hanuman Chalisa Parayana & Stotras

Collective powerful parayana of Sri Hanuman Chalisa and sacred stotras for fearlessness and mental clarity.

11:45 AM

Interactive Youth Satsang

Engaging group discussion: Translating ancient spiritual principles into modern everyday youth life.

Farewell Mahaprasada Lunch

Traditional Sunday mahaprasada feast shared with all fellow yatris.

02:30 PM

Reflections, Photos & Blessings

Sharing our yatra memories, blessings from mentors, and commemorative group photos.

Departure in Volvo Bus Towards Home

Boarding our Volvo bus with elevated minds, peaceful hearts, and lifelong spiritual friendships.

  • Age Limit: 18 to 35 years exclusively.
  • Attire: Traditional, modest Indian clothing suitable for temple premises and Goshala seva.
  • Essentials: Reusable water bottle, personal toiletries, and any personal routine medications.

  • Identification: A valid government photo ID card (Aadhaar / Driving License).
